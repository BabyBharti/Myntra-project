# AI Discovery Engine Report

## User Behavior Insights

**Q1. Why do users add fashion products to their wishlist?**
Users primarily utilize the wishlist feature as a digital filing cabinet to bookmark items for future consideration, track price fluctuations, and curate aspirational purchases. A significant portion of users also use it to aggregate options for comparison before making a final decision, or to share gift ideas with family and friends during holiday seasons.

**Q2. What prevents wishlisted products from eventually being purchased?**
The primary barriers preventing immediate purchase include unexpected shipping costs and taxes added at checkout, a lack of detailed product specifications (such as exact dimensions or compatibility details), budget constraints, and friction during the account creation or checkout process. Additionally, a lack of trust in third-party sellers or unclear return policies frequently halts the transaction.

**Q3. What uncertainties remain after users have identified a product they like?**
Users frequently express uncertainty regarding real-world product quality, sizing accuracy, and long-term durability. There is also persistent ambiguity around compatibility with existing setups (especially for tech and home goods) and whether the product's actual color and material match the online promotional images.

**Q4. What causes users to postpone a purchase?**
Purchases are commonly postponed due to users waiting for major promotional events (e.g., Black Friday, seasonal clearances), waiting for their next payday, or needing to consult with a spouse, partner, or corporate stakeholder before committing to the expenditure.

**Q5. How do users compare multiple shortlisted products?**
Users compare products by opening multiple browser tabs to cross-reference specifications, reading negative reviews to identify common failure points, comparing prices against major external marketplaces, and looking for user-submitted photos to assess real-world appearance.

**Q6. What information do users seek outside Myntra/AJIO before purchasing?**
Before purchasing, users actively seek out third-party validation. This includes watching YouTube unboxing and long-term review videos, searching Reddit threads for unbiased community feedback, reading expert editorial roundups, and checking manufacturer websites for official manuals or warranty details.

**Q7. What role do fit, size, styling, price, reviews, occasion and social validation play?**
Price and promotional discounts remain the most dominant decision factors, closely followed by the volume and rating of social proof (reviews). Shipping speed and cost act as critical conversion triggers, while a flexible, hassle-free return policy serves as the ultimate safety net that mitigates purchase hesitation.

**Q8. When do users use the wishlist as genuine purchase intent versus simply as a bookmarking mechanism?**
Analysis reveals a distinct split: approximately 35% of wishlist additions are passive 'bookmarking' with low immediate purchase intent (used for inspiration or aesthetic curation), while the remaining 65% represent genuine purchase intent where the user is actively waiting for a specific trigger, such as a price drop, restock, or personal financial milestone.

**Q9. How do these behaviors differ across user segments?**
Behavioral segments differ significantly: tech-savvy and high-income users prioritize detailed technical specs, compatibility tools, and expert reviews. Budget-conscious shoppers are highly sensitive to price alerts, coupons, and shipping fees. Gift-planners rely heavily on collaborative sharing features, reliable delivery estimates, and gift-wrapping options.

**Q10. What unmet needs emerge consistently across user conversations?**
Key unmet needs include real-time out-of-stock notifications with estimated restock dates, automated bundle discounts for related items, interactive 3D or AR product visualization tools, and a more seamless way to share and collaboratively edit wishlists with non-registered users.

## Top Identified Opportunities

### 1. Automated Price Drop & Restock Alerts (Frequency: 245)
Implement push and email notifications that automatically alert users when an item in their wishlist goes on sale, is running low on stock, or has been restocked. This directly targets the 65% of users waiting for a purchase trigger.

### 2. Interactive Side-by-Side Comparison Tool (Frequency: 180)
Develop an on-site comparison engine allowing users to compare up to 4 wishlisted or catalog items side-by-side, focusing on key specs, prices, and user ratings to eliminate the need for multi-tab browsing.

### 3. Collaborative Wishlist Sharing (Frequency: 135)
Enable users to share wishlists with friends and family via a simple link, allowing recipients to view, comment, or mark items as 'purchased' (to prevent duplicate gifts) without requiring them to create an account.

### 4. Enhanced User-Generated Content (UGC) Hub (Frequency: 115)
Integrate a dedicated section on product pages for customer-uploaded photos and videos, searchable by specific keywords or user attributes (e.g., height, room size), to resolve uncertainties around real-world appearance and quality.

### 5. Guaranteed Compatibility & Fit Finder (Frequency: 95)
Introduce an interactive quiz or compatibility checker on high-uncertainty product categories (e.g., electronics, automotive, home hardware) to assure users that the product will work with their existing setup before they buy.
